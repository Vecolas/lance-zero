# Endgames Pages

## `/finais`

Purpose:
> browse endgame concepts and techniques.

Header:
```text
Finais
Aprenda a reconhecer posições, encontrar planos e converter vantagens.
```

Use mini-board cards by:
- principle;
- technique;
- material class;
- defense/conversion.

Do not visually imply that these are opening lines.

Card examples:
- Oposição
- Regra do quadrado
- Rei + Torre contra Rei
- Lucena
- Philidor

## `/finais/[slug]`

Peer views:
- Visão geral
- Aprender
- Praticar
- Posições típicas
- Erros comuns
- Progresso

For theoretical techniques, `Técnica` may replace/augment `Aprender`.

### Learn mode

Do not present a long move list as the primary panel.

Use:
```text
board
+ current principle/question
+ demonstration
+ micro-decision
```

The page should visually reinforce:
```text
recognize
→ choose principle
→ plan
→ calculate
→ execute
```

### Practice/play-out

The board persists across multiple moves.

Display:
- objective;
- status;
- minimal relevant feedback.

Do not celebrate after the first correct move if the actual objective is mate/promotion/draw.

### Position variants

Show several mini-boards or a compact position selector.

The user should understand that the technique generalizes across positions.

### Tablebase/engine information

Keep under an advanced/details area unless essential.

Human explanation appears first.
