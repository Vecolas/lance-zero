# Other Core Pages

## Game Review

Primary job:
> understand a small number of meaningful decisions.

Use:
```text
board | explanation / critical moments
timeline below or adjacent
```

Do not make engine graph visually louder than the explanation.

Highlight a few critical moments rather than every small eval fluctuation.

## Calculation

Primary job:
> externalize the user's calculation process.

Order:
1. board;
2. candidate moves;
3. opponent response;
4. line;
5. evaluation/reflection.

Do not reveal engine answer while candidates are being generated.

## Progress

Answer:
- what improved?;
- what remains weak?;
- what should I train next?

Prefer a small number of meaningful visualizations.

Do not build an analytics dashboard full of unrelated charts.

## Profile

Keep simple:
- avatar;
- username;
- display name;
- chess profile/preferences.

Do not combine security controls into profile if they deserve a separate route.

## Security

Use a quiet, trustworthy layout.

Actions like:
- password;
- MFA;
- sessions;
- delete account

must be clearly separated.

Destructive account deletion must not visually resemble ordinary settings.
