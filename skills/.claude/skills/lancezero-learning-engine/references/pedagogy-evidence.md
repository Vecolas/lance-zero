# Pedagogy Evidence → Product Rules

## Chess deliberate practice

Charness et al. (2005) found serious individual study was the strongest measured predictor of chess skill in two samples.

Product translation:
- training should target identified deficiencies;
- do not optimize merely for time spent.

Source:
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/acp.1106

## Retrieval practice

Testing/retrieval can strengthen later retention more than restudy.

Product translation:
- require attempt before explanation when reviewing learned material.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26151629/

## Distributed practice

Meta-analytic evidence supports spaced over massed practice.

Product translation:
- resurface fragile patterns across time.

Sources:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40564553/

## Worked examples

Worked examples reduce unnecessary cognitive load for novices when examples are well chosen. Benefits diminish as expertise grows.

Product translation:
- teach first;
- then remove scaffolding;
- avoid permanently turning every exercise into a walkthrough.

## Caution

General learning research informs design but does not prove a single optimal chess-training percentage. Treat curriculum weights as product hypotheses to validate.
